# Job Scoring Skill

Use a transparent scoring model to prioritize opportunities for review.

Suggested dimensions:

- Role alignment: 0–30
- Technical skill alignment: 0–25
- Location/work-mode alignment: 0–15
- Seniority alignment: 0–15
- Compensation evidence: 0–10
- Freshness/source quality: 0–5

Total: 100.

Scoring is an internal prioritization mechanism, not a claim about the candidate's worth or employability.

Important:

- Do not award compensation points when compensation is unknown.
- Do not convert unknown information into a positive assumption.
- Keep the component breakdown with every score.
- Never fabricate missing evidence.
